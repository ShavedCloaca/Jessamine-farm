Our story

Where we started, what we found,
and where we're headed.

Jaqueline and Anthony Hicks made Jessamine Farm their family home in 1978 — opening the touring caravan park and bed & breakfast to help support the family. Today, the farm is in the hands of the next two generations.

1978 — the beginning

Where we started

Jaqueline and Anthony Hicks made Jessamine Farm their family home in 1978. To help support her family, Jaqueline opened the touring caravan park and bed and breakfast.

A local legend

Stories we tell

While renovating the property, stones were found in the historic garden wall — carved with skulls and crossed picks and shovels, marking a grave. Records turned up the last will of a local smuggler, who asked his friends at Jessamine Farm for "a good and decent burial."

Today & tomorrow

Where we're going

Jaqueline has now taken a step back from the business, handing the farm over to her daughter Stephanie and granddaughter Emily — who are carrying on her work, and starting a few new ventures of their own.

Jessamine Farm

A family-run touring caravan park and pop-up campsite in the heart of Romney Marsh — welcoming visitors since 1978.
