Jessamine Farm Site Rules

We want you to have a wonderful, relaxing stay with us. To keep the farm safe, peaceful, and enjoyable for everyone, we kindly ask that you follow these friendly guidelines during your visit.

⛺ Arrival & Departure

  • Check-in is between 2:00 PM and 6:00 PM. Please ensure you are set up before dusk. (No arrivals after 6:30 PM).
  • Check-out is by 11:00 AM. (A £10 fee applies for late departures).
  • Early arrivals from 9:00 AM can be arranged in advance for £10.

🌙 Peace & Quiet

  • Quiet hours are strictly observed between 11:00 PM and 8:00 AM.
  • Music must be kept at a low, reasonable level and switched off completely by 11:00 PM.
  • Alcoholic drinks should only be consumed in the immediate vicinity of your pitch.
  • Loud or anti-social behaviour is not tolerated and may result in being asked to leave without a refund.

🚗 Vehicles & Parking

  • The site speed limit is strictly 5 mph for everyone's safety.
  • Your pitch allowance includes one sleeping vehicle and one car.
  • Any additional cars or visitor vehicles must be parked in the main car park (£5 per night/day).
  • Charging Electric Vehicles (EVs) from our pitch hook-ups is strictly prohibited due to fire safety risks (a £50 penalty applies).

🔥 Campfires & Safety

  • BBQs are welcome, but must be used on slabs/bricks (ask the team to borrow some!).
  • Only fire pits provided by Jessamine Farm (on legs) are permitted. Ground fires are strictly prohibited.
  • Please use our provided firewood; collecting dead wood from the farm disrupts natural habitats.
  • Gas cookers, naked flames, and charging large lithium-ion batteries (e-bikes/scooters) inside tents are strictly prohibited.

🐾 Dog Owners

  • Dogs must be kept on a lead at all times on the property.
  • Please bag and bin all waste responsibly in the provided bins.
  • Dogs must not be taken into the washrooms or left unattended.

🪁 Kids, Play & Nature

  • Children must be supervised at all times and accompanied by an adult in the washrooms.
  • For safety, please don't allow playing near ditches, pond banks, or farm equipment.
  • Kite flying, drones, water balloons, and fireworks/lanterns are prohibited for safety and wildlife protection.
  • Paddling pools are not permitted to prevent water waste and grass damage.

♻️ Facilities & Waste

  • Please leave the communal washrooms clean after use; cleaning products are provided for your convenience.
  • Please use the correct recycling bins for glass, cardboard, cans, and plastics.
  • Only dispose of regular household rubbish in the bins. Do not leave broken camping equipment behind.
  • Wi-Fi is available to purchase if you need it (£2/day).

👋 Day Visitors

  • Day guests are welcome, but must sign in at reception for fire and safety compliance.
  • Please let us know before your guests arrive. Unannounced visitors will be refused entry.
  • Visitors arriving by car must use the car park (£5 per day).

If you have any questions, run into issues, or just need a recommendation for dinner, please pop into the office or call us at 01797 362 850. Have a wonderful stay!
