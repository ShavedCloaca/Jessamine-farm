Things to do

The marsh, and everything
around it.

Old Romney sits at the centre of it all — steam railways, safari parks, ancient bone crypts, and a few very good places to eat.

The Tipsy Broomstick

Open to the public, our café serves specialty coffee and sweet treats, with a menu that changes with the seasons. The Tipsy Broomstick is open for 3 nights a week serving alcohol.

Puff, Paddy and Misty, the rescue ponies

Jessamine's rescued ponies are a gentle and friendly presence on the farm, offering visitors a chance to get up close and personal with these beautiful animals. Keep an eye out for their carers Kirtsy and Bea giving them a walk around the farm.

The Farm Orchard

Our orchard is home to a variety of fruit trees, including apples, pears, and plums. It's a great place to pick your own fruit in the summer months. Its also a lovely place to walk your dogs or enjoy a picnic

Brenzett Aeronautical Museum

A collection of wartime equipment, aircraft-crash remains and memorabilia, gathered since the museum's formation in 1972 as a tribute to all who served.

Visit site →

RH&DR Miniature Railway

Kent's "mainline in miniature" — one-third scale steam and diesel locomotives run 13½ miles from Hythe to Dungeness, stopping at four stations, each near a beach.

Visit site →

Port Lympne Reserve

A safari-style day out across hundreds of acres of Kentish savannah, at the Port Lympne Hotel & Reserve.

Visit site →

Dungeness Lighthouse

A Grade II listed lighthouse, first listed in 1992, that has recently celebrated its centenary looking out over the shingle.

Visit site →

The Ossuary, St Leonard's Church

Britain's largest and best-preserved bone crypt — around 1,000 skulls across four bays, plus a 7.5m stack of bones reassembled in 1910.

Visit site →

Elham Valley Line Trust

A registered charity, run entirely by volunteers since 1984, preserving railway history and countryside crafts through its museum and craft centre.

Visit site →

The Coach House

A restaurant and bar in New Romney, with a Mediterranean twist on the menu.

Ruzzo

A family-run Italian in New Romney, known for a warm welcome and full-flavoured cooking — or bring Italy to your pitch with delivery.

Curry Lounge

You can't go wrong with a curry — a local favourite for a takeaway night in.

Oasis Cafe

Right next door to the farm — a classic British cafe for all your fry-up needs.

The Bell Inn

A traditional pub in Ivychurch, a short drive away, with good pub grub and often live music and games.

Mama's Hut

Street food to die for, a short walk from the farm or delivered straight to you if you fancy a night off cooking.

Cycle routes

Old Romney sits on part of National Cycle Network Route 2 and Route 11 — but with so many mapped and unmapped lanes nearby, you could ride for hours and never run out of sights.

Walking routes

We're lucky to sit at the heart of Romney Marsh, with fantastic local trails. Farmers here are required to leave field access for the public, which turns a walk — dogs included — from a chore into a real pleasure.
